FLAIR — Flood Level Assessment & Intelligent Response

IoT-Based Real-Time Flood Monitoring & Early Warning Prediction System

FLAIR is an end-to-end IoT early warning ecosystem engineered to observe, analyze, and predict environmental parameters in high-risk flood zones. Deploying decentralized sensor nodes across critical water channels, FLAIR captures real-time telemetry—including water depth, precipitation, ambient temperature, and humidity. Transmitted securely to a centralized cloud dashboard, the data powers trend analysis and automated alerting pipelines to assist emergency management teams in proactive risk mitigation.

Water Level Monitoring: Precise real-time hydrologic tracking using ultrasonic sensor telemetry.
Environmental Telemetry: Continuous monitoring of humidity and temperature metrics.
Early Flood Prediction: Predictive analytics based on historical sensor data trends.
Cloud Dashboard: Web dashboard displaying live visual telemetry analytics.
Automated Alerts: Incident alert dispatch triggers when sensor data exceeds safety limits.
Scalable Architecture: Multi-node sensor topology engineered for multi-region spatial coverage.
